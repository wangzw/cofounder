# SKILL.md "Configuration & Subagent Files" lists 5 sub-agent prompts but skill ships 8 — orchestrator-facing inventory diverges from on-disk file set

## Finding

`SKILL.md` lines 302–313 (the "Configuration & Subagent Files" section, the
orchestrator-facing inventory of available sub-agent prompts) lists only **5**
sub-agent files:

```
- **Sub-agent prompts**:
  - `generate/writer-subagent.md`
  - `review/cross-reviewer-subagent.md`
  - `revise/per-issue-reviser-subagent.md`
  - `shared/summarizer-subagent.md`
  - `shared/judge-subagent.md`
```

But the skill **actually ships 8 sub-agent prompts** on disk — confirmed by:

```
$ ls generate/*-subagent.md review/*-subagent.md revise/*-subagent.md shared/*-subagent.md
generate/domain-consultant-subagent.md
generate/planner-subagent.md
generate/writer-subagent.md
review/adversarial-reviewer-subagent.md
review/cross-reviewer-subagent.md
revise/per-issue-reviser-subagent.md
shared/judge-subagent.md
shared/summarizer-subagent.md
```

The three missing entries — `generate/domain-consultant-subagent.md`,
`generate/planner-subagent.md`, `review/adversarial-reviewer-subagent.md` — are
all referenced elsewhere in the bundle:

- `generate/from-scratch.md` Step 4 (line 59): `Dispatches: generate/domain-consultant-subagent.md`
- `generate/from-scratch.md` Step 5 (line 65): `Dispatches: generate/planner-subagent.md`
- `review/index.md` Step 4 (line 81): `Dispatches: review/adversarial-reviewer-subagent.md`
- `generate/planner-subagent.md` (line 64–72) lists all 8 in its `add` list.
- `common/templates/skill-md-template.md` lines 130–137 explicitly enumerate **all 8** sub-agent paths in its "Configuration & Subagent Files" reference shape.

CR-L04 (criteria-internally-consistent, applied here as cross-reference
consistency between SKILL.md and the rest of the bundle) requires these
authoritative inventories to agree. They do not.

The cross-reviewer's R6-V001-001 / R6-V001-002 / R6-V002-001 / R6-V002-002 / R6-V002-003
flagged the *content* of three sub-agent prompts (DOMAIN_FILL stubs in
domain-consultant, adversarial-reviewer, per-issue-reviser). None flagged the
**inventory mismatch in SKILL.md itself**. This is a separate failure mode:
even when the three stub prompts are filled, SKILL.md as currently written
would still under-advertise the skill's sub-agent surface to a reader of the
orchestrator-facing entry point.

## Why this matters (skill-forge–specific anti-pattern)

A reader navigating the skill from `SKILL.md` (the documented entry point per
the skill plugin loader) sees only 5 sub-agents and may reasonably conclude
that `domain-consultant`, `planner`, and `adversarial-reviewer` were
intentionally omitted from this skill — perhaps because PRD work is too
deterministic to need a domain-consultant, or because a different orchestration
omits the planner. That reading is wrong: the planner IS dispatched
unconditionally in `generate/from-scratch.md` Step 5. The skill cannot run
generate-from-scratch without it.

This is the same class of failure as CR-S04 (subagent-file-inventory) at the
*structural* level — but at the *orchestrator-facing-documentation* level, no
script-tier check exists. CR-L04 is the appropriate criterion.
