# CR-L11

domain-glossary documents structural-lint and design-review outputs landing in `.reviews/` only, ignoring the in-pipeline `.review/round-N/issues/` path

## Reasoning

`common/domain-glossary.md` defines `structural-lint`:
> "Output lands in `.reviews/LINT-{timestamp}.md`."

And `design-review`:
> "Findings land in `.reviews/REVIEW-{timestamp}.md`."

This is only HALF of where these artifacts actually land. Per `from-scratch.md` Step 9, in-pipeline structural lint findings land at `<target>/.review/round-1/issues/` (no S, with NNN sequence numbering, not timestamps). Per `from-scratch.md` Step 10 + new-version.md Step 11, in-pipeline cross-reviewer/adversarial findings land at the same in-pipeline path. The `.reviews/`-with-S path is the `--review` mode artefact directory, used by the end-user read-only review flow (`review/index.md` Step 3, Step 4). They are two separate output channels for the same conceptual artifact (lint or review issue).

The glossary is the canonical reference both writer and reviewer sub-agents consult when disambiguating terms (per `generate/domain-consultant-subagent.md` Input Contract). A glossary that names only one of two output paths makes "where does my finding go" a coin-flip:

- A writer trying to predict where its self-review FAIL row lands cannot tell which directory the cross-reviewer will write its escalated issue to.
- A reviser invoked from `--revise` mode (`.reviews/`) versus from in-pipeline lint loop (`.review/round-N/issues/`) needs to know which directory to consume from; the glossary gives only one answer.

The two-phase quality gate term in the glossary similarly says "Lint findings land in `.reviews/LINT-*.md`; semantic findings in `.reviews/REVIEW-*.md`" — same omission.

## Suggested Fix

Update the three glossary entries (`structural-lint`, `design-review`, `two-phase quality gate`) to name BOTH output paths with a "depending on mode" qualifier:

- "Output lands in `<design-dir>/.reviews/LINT-<NNN>.md` (`--review` mode) or `<design-dir>/.review/round-<N>/issues/LINT-NNN.md` (in-pipeline generate/new-version mode)."
- Same shape for `design-review` REVIEW-NNN.md.
- Same for `two-phase quality gate`.

The naming convention difference (timestamp vs. NNN sequence) is a separate downstream concern documented in `review/index.md`; the glossary's job is to surface that two paths exist.
